## Suggested contribution paragraph

ZeroRun is an execution component for AI coding tools, not a new caching algorithm. It exposes repository-bound test execution through Python, CLI and MCP interfaces, restricting whole-task reuse to previously successful deterministic, result-only commands with revalidated declared inputs and runtime identity. Its read-only hit path avoids execution-snapshot construction while retaining authenticated lookup and content checks; misses and explicit verification retain isolated execution. The contribution is an inspectable implementation, explicit refusal boundaries, and source-bound reproduction material separating correctness checks, eligible hits and complete-sequence cost. This is a different deployment and contract from feature/TTL-guided tool-result caching and trajectory-prefix reuse in RL rollouts, not evidence of superiority over either. Cached success is not a reproduced test transcript; the evaluation does not establish unchanged autonomous-agent behavior. [ToolCaching](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.15335v1), [TVCache](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10986v1).

## Reviewer-facing limits and editorial decisions

- Remove any claim of the first cache for AI tools, first state-aware tool cache, or first avoidance of expensive snapshots. The closest preprints directly preclude those broad claims.
- State the software's AI connection as a tool-execution integration layer. Do not imply that ZeroRun trains a model, accelerates inference, improves reasoning, or preserves task success without corresponding experiments.
- Do not compare published headline speedups numerically: the tools have different workloads, denominators and execution contracts. Our direct and snapshot-first arms answer within-system questions, not a head-to-head comparison against these systems.
- Public command traces can motivate workload relevance; repeated command text alone does not prove identical inputs, cache eligibility, safe replay, or realized latency savings.
- Complete declared input closure and determinism remain assumptions requiring review. Laboratory fixtures do not authorize production reuse. The current evaluated result-only interface requires callers to accept explicit reuse provenance instead of historical stdout/stderr or fresh test evidence.
- eidos was inspected through the publisher abstract and bibliographic metadata only because the full text was inaccessible. Do not infer implementation details or absence of caching from that limited view. SoftwareX publication is category precedent, not an acceptance-rate estimate.
- Cite ToolCaching and TVCache as 2026 arXiv preprints. ToolCaching's HTML contains unfilled conference-template metadata; do not reproduce those placeholders as a real venue.

## Local implementation anchors
