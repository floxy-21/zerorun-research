## Journal requirements

The [current author instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/10664/submission-guidelines) were retrieved on 29 October 2026. They require editable sources, a 150–250-word abstract, 4–6 keywords, author/contact information, relevant declarations and a data-availability statement. The journal uses single-blind review. Mathematical papers can use LaTeX; submission files should be flat rather than in subfolders. Substantive LLM use must be disclosed, and the human author remains accountable. The standard subscription publication route has no submission/publication fee; optional open access is separate. No paid publication choice is made here.

The [journal scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/10664/aims-and-scope) supports original and replicated empirical studies with practical relevance. The [published editorial criteria](https://emsejournal.github.io/special_issues/2025_SE_LLMs.html) emphasize relevance, significance, technical quality, scholarship and presentation. That special issue's call is not treated as an open deadline or the selected article type.

Recent methodological comparators inspected by the fresh contribution reviewer include [performance-test cost/coverage](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-025-10712-3), [AutoTCP](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-026-10862-y), and [automated development workflows](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10664-026-10950-z). These support explicit research questions, transparent selection and failure accounting, and separation of guided demonstrations from automated workflow evidence. Their project counts are not invented minimum sample requirements.

## Stable publication-critical gates

These gates merge overlapping issues from the three early reviews. They are fixed for this study; optional extensions do not become new requirements without a demonstrated defect.

1. **Distinct empirical contribution:** inspected closest-work comparison and a frozen question that yields a deployment-relevant finding beyond faster conditional hits. A negative result is acceptable if the evidence answers that question.
2. **Qualified correctness:** explicit task semantics, source-state qualification, independent full-target execution checks, and retained refusals/errors. No universal soundness inferred from zero observed disagreement.
3. **Complete cost:** acquisition, preparation, qualification/renewal, producer, consumer, maintenance and any required operational audit included in the declared scenario. Separate machine elapsed, AI-assisted operator elapsed and unknown human effort. Report break-even sensitivity without replacing unknowns by zero.
4. **Fair alternatives and opportunities:** fresh execution and an independent minimal authenticated receipt baseline, plus clearly separated observed-request replay and imposed repeated handoffs. Same runtime, target, result semantics and resource limits wherever comparable.
5. **Prospective analysis:** pilot versus main selection recorded, fixed ordering/replication/analysis before main measurement, paired uncertainty at the correct unit, complete accounting for failures and host interruptions.
6. **Executable package:** clean installation, rerunnable acquisition/experiment/analysis, raw data and provenance, source-linked central results, verified references, licenses and checksums. Frozen historical evidence remains separate.
7. **Journal and manuscript closure:** latest eligible ranking evidence; current format/declarations; source/PDF and supplements; fresh complete-paper reviews and an evidence-linked response closing every material issue. Exact final author approval and portal submission are distinct from technical submission readiness.

Optional improvements: additional clients, independent human replication, broader adoption, second hardware platform and more repositories after the fixed study. They are required only if the final paper makes claims needing them.
