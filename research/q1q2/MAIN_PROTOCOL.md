# Frozen main evaluation contract

This protocol incorporates the three finite clarifications in `reviews/early/main-protocol-focused-review.md` before main timing. The primary journal and seven publication gates remain in JOURNAL_STANDARD.md. Main candidates and pins are in MAIN_SELECTION.json, the complete API sample in MAIN_REQUESTS.json, and the randomized balanced order in MAIN_SCHEDULE.json. Two pilot projects and their three attempts are excluded from main inference.

The estimand is conditional on the six purposively chosen unit-test targets, the realized supported subset, the declared runtime/isolation policy, and two realized hosted sessions. The tasks run the full `tests/` directory under Python 3.10 and locked dependencies, with a fixed Hypothesis seed where the plugin is installed. They do not reproduce upstream lint, documentation, optional acceleration extras or cross-platform jobs. No assertions or tests are removed. Preflight failures remain in the selected-target denominator and may be repaired for a concrete missing prerequisite with versioned evidence.

Each supported target has twelve paired blocks: all six permutations of fresh, receipt and ZeroRun once per session, shuffled with seed 20261029 before any main result. Sessions run sequentially. Each arm gets a new private store and source materialization. The fresh arm performs one producer and four consumers. Each reuse arm additionally executes one independent full-tree qualification oracle before its producer; the producer's observed outcome must match that oracle before historical consumers proceed. This records a concrete automated qualification policy under the stated outcome-determinism assumption. It is neither a proof of determinism nor a substitute for human review.

Primary total at N consumers is source-materialization time plus arm-authority setup plus the additional qualification-oracle materialization, setup and invocation time (reuse arms only) plus one producer and the first N consumers, including invocation, response serialization, lookups, execution, publication, cleanup and gate scans. N=1 is primary; N=0,2,4 are dependent secondary prefixes. The prequalified-source sensitivity omits the additional oracle, explicitly assuming that cost was paid earlier for exactly the same qualified state. Runtime/image preparation and project acquisition are common first-use costs displayed separately and allocated identically to each counterfactual arm. Every attempted preparation and failure cost remains in the all-attempt ledger. Research-only extra boundary challenges are not charged to production. Measurement repetitions are not production reuse horizons and cannot amortize operator review.

Primary comparisons are fresh minus ZeroRun and fresh minus receipt, and the direct implementation comparison is ZeroRun minus receipt. Positive differences favor the second named system. For each target and N, resample complete paired blocks within each session (six per session), retain paired arm values and all N prefixes, use 10,000 draws with seed 20261029, and report pointwise 95% percentile intervals plus each session's estimate. No family-wise significance claims are made. For a practical 5% improvement claim over fresh, bootstrap the statistic `mean(T_fresh - T_arm) - 0.05 * mean(T_fresh)` inside each draw; its lower 2.5th percentile must exceed zero. Also show raw signed differences and ratios; do not select only significant targets. Intervals describe repeat variability conditional on these tasks and sessions, not independent repository or hardware samples.

The available additional-review budget is the signed measured fresh-minus-reuse total under each explicitly labeled scenario. Unknown human active time, elapsed delay and money are separate variables; no human labor estimate is inferred from model actions. Report negative budgets and undefined break-even points. The cost identity is an accounting device, not claimed as a new theorem or learned admission algorithm. A calibration prediction based on session 0 mean producer, qualification and consumer components may be compared with session 1 totals without refitting; it is temporal cost validation on the same fixed tasks, not unseen-task prediction.

B3 uses the latest snapshot of 84 selected workflow-run records, including eight natsort records marked run_attempt=2. Ordering by created_at is record-creation ordering; intermediate retry attempts and their chronology are unknown. The replay is a counterfactual standardized sequence of these retained records, not a reconstruction of actual CI execution order or actual historical-answer demand. The no-observed-Actions project remains in the six-project denominator. Every record receives acquired, unavailable, unsupported, failed, refused, successful-miss or eligible-hit disposition; command text and SHA alone never establish a hit. Action-required and cancelled records remain marked even when their source can be replayed. No extra unchanged requests are inserted into this record replay.

Known trust limits remain explicit: trusted host and operator, quiescent owned source, no adversarial daemon, conditional outcome determinism and result-only semantics. Exact-state authority records the scope of a decision and cannot discover semantic nondeterminism. Direct full-tree and staged executions must have equal nonempty node/outcome digests for each admitted source; metadata-dependent or omitted-input counterexamples are qualification failures. The native 4,000-byte response tail is preserved and matched by the receipt/fresh reference; an external full oracle capture checks the compact digest outside timed requests.

The fixed target order is the order in MAIN_SELECTION.json in each session; arm orders alone are randomized within target and session. This choice is frozen before B2 and supports conditional per-target comparisons, not a randomized project-order effect. The receipt comparator drains output with bounded 4,000-byte tails. The external full oracle capture is capped at 50 MB and must contain the complete digest input; exceeding that cap is a failed qualification.
